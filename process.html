Return to battle

Build notes

Combat revision

Removed target-practice challenges. Added bounded movement for both players and the computer, expanded world width from 1440 to 2160, and converted the interface to English.

Simulation

Fixed 120 Hz projectile physics, destructible heightmap, wind, ammunition, turn handoff and victory detection. Movement uses small collision-checked steps and a shared per-turn distance allowance.

Sound

Local CC0 sound samples replace synthesized tones. Firing, engine movement, ground impacts and direct armor hits have separate effects. Audio is stereo-positioned and stops when menus pause the battle.

Verification

Automated checks cover the 1.5× map width, movement budget and reversal, boundaries and walls, tank collisions, damage and craters, projectile termination and cross-map reachability for both sides in 18 terrain/wind configurations.

Limitations

Local shared-screen multiplayer, not networked play. Terrain supports craters but not caves. Native fullscreen and audio require a user interaction.
