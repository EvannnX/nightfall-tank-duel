Game rules

Combat only

Local two-player duel or a computer opponent. Both tanks start with 100 HP. Destroy the enemy to win; simultaneous destruction is a draw.

Movement

60 m per turn, shared between forward and reverse. Hold A / D or use the Move buttons. Firing ends the turn. Steep walls and other tanks block movement.

Battlefield

A 2160 × 670 world: 1.5 times the original width. Wind affects projectiles, and explosions permanently reshape the terrain during a match.

Weapons

Shell: unlimited, 36 direct damage. Heavy: 3 rounds, 57 damage. Cluster: 3 rounds, three projectiles with 23 damage each. Digger: 2 rounds, 24 damage and a larger crater. Blast damage falls with distance and includes friendly fire.

Presentation

Full-window Canvas battlefield with generated landscape and rock textures, a visible mode-selection portal, and local sample-based firing, engine, terrain-impact and armor-hit sounds. No online multiplayer or account is required.
