SESSION 2 · WORKBOOK & SYSTEM

From experience to a playable system

The original workbook export remains available as brief.md. This page makes the implemented system, data relationships, feedback, challenge range, and later design drift explicit.

Design Core

Domain
Turn-based artillery: estimating a ballistic path under uncertain wind and changing terrain.
Learning shift
From guessing a shot to calibrating the next shot using visible evidence.
Core action
Observe → reposition → select weapon → set angle and power → fire → interpret → adjust.
Emotional target
Tension before commitment, surprise during flight, and competence when a correction works.
Success
Use movement, weapons, terrain, and trajectory evidence to reduce the opponent from 100 HP to zero.

System Graph

The graph separates player-readable evidence from hidden calculations. Every calculation returns a visible or audible consequence so that the next decision can be more informed.

Challenge Progression

1 · Night Valley

Ability: basic angle and power calibration.
Variation: moderate wind, elevated starts.
Just-right result: a near miss clearly indicates more or less range.

2 · Broken Ridge

Ability: high arcs, repositioning, or terrain alteration.
Variation: central obstruction and light wind.
Failure: a blocked shot still exposes the needed clearance.

3 · Rolling Hills

Ability: compensate for horizontal drift and longer flight time.
Variation: open terrain and stronger wind.
Success: use a previous trail to correct both range and drift.

Feedback Translation

The simulation does not show formulas during play. It translates them into barrel rotation, an initial-direction guide, shell motion, wind drift, persistent trails, explosions, sound, health loss, and terrain deformation. The player learns relationships through comparison: “that angle and power under that wind landed here.”

Concept Drift Record

Original workbook conceptPocket Artillery Lab: a bright, warm, minimal 2D experiment with three target challenges, no active tank movement, and synthesized sound.
Implemented conceptNightfall: a realistic night-time competitive duel with VS AI and local PVP, 60 m movement per turn, three competitive maps, local sample-based audio, and a complete game flow.
Why it changedThe student explicitly requested full opposition, limited movement, a larger map, realistic presentation and sound, English-only UI, responsive display, and full onboarding/setup.
What remained stableThe core ballistic learning loop, wind, angle, power, visible shot evidence, destructible terrain, and learning through correction.
