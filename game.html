Firing ends movement for this turn. Gravity curves every shell downward. Explosions damage nearby tanks—including yours—and dig permanent craters. Reduce enemy HP to zero to win.

Controls

Objective & turns

Both tanks start with 100 HP. Destroy the opponent to win. If both are destroyed by the same volley, the battle is a draw. Move, choose a weapon, aim, then fire once. After the shells land, control passes to the other side.

Movement

Hold A / D or the Move buttons; tap a button to move 6 m. Your 60 m allowance resets at the start of your turn. Going forward then backward consumes distance in both directions. Steep slopes, walls and tanks block movement. You cannot move after firing.

Angle, power & trajectory

0° points right, 90° straight up, 180° left. Angle is measured against the world, not the slope under your tank. Use ← / → to adjust it, and ↑ / ↓ for power. Sliders work with mouse or touch. More power means more launch speed, not more damage.

The short white dotted line starts exactly at the muzzle and shows initial direction only—not the complete flight path. Gravity bends the shell down; wind changes its horizontal motion. Older dotted trails show where previous shots actually went.

Wind

→ pushes shells right; ← pushes them left. The number is horizontal acceleration in world m/s², not wind speed. Wind changes at each turn and stays fixed while shells fly. High, slow shots spend longer in the air and are affected more. Heavy shells resist wind better; Cluster shells drift more.

Weapons & damage

Press 1–4 or select a weapon. Shell: unlimited, 36 direct-hit damage. Heavy: 3 shots, 57 damage, slower launch and less wind drift. Cluster: 3 shots, three shells spread by ±5°, each with 23 damage. Digger: 2 shots, 24 damage with a wider crater. These are per-player supplies.

Blast damage falls with distance and can hit your own tank. Terrain stays destroyed for the rest of the match. Tanks settle onto the new ground. Your movement allowance does not refill when a crater forms.

AI versus local PVP

VS AI: you control red; the computer controls blue and takes its own turn automatically. LOCAL PVP: two players share one keyboard or touch screen; pass control when the turn label changes. This is not online multiplayer. Both modes use the same health, movement and weapon rules.

Pause, sound & display

Space fires. P or the menu / portal pauses the battle. Menus stop simulation and sounds. Use Sound to mute effects. The fullscreen button toggles fullscreen; Esc exits browser fullscreen. Portrait screens use an automatic camera that follows the active tank and flying shell. The minimap shows both tanks. Tap Overview for the full map, or Follow tank to return. Landscape screens show the whole battlefield without stretching.
