# Sound assets

All files are bundled locally under CC0 1.0.

- fire.ogg, armor.ogg, impact.ogg: Thimras, [Battle at sea](https://opengameart.org/content/battle-at-sea). Originals: cannon_fire_1.ogg, cannon_hit_cannon_1.ogg, cannon_hit_wall_no_splash.ogg.
- engine.mp3: pauliuw, [Engine sounds (2)](https://opengameart.org/content/engine-sounds2), original engine_sound.mp3.

Sample-based effects, with runtime volume, stereo positioning and playback-rate changes. These are designed sound effects; not claimed to be field recordings of a specific tank.
